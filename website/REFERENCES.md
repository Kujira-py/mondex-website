## Existing local art

Card and Pokémon image source records are copied into `public/assets/provenance.json`. Originals live in `../mobile/assets/collector/`. Font: `../mobile/assets/fonts/Onest`, OFL copied alongside local font. Logo: `../mobile/assets/brand/orbit.svg`; 3D shape uses its original paths.

No supplied app screenshot, screenshot crop or new app-interface mockup is used in the final site. Pokémon artwork ownership remains with its respective rights holders; the local repository's provenance is not a general marketing license. Clearance is a publication prerequisite.
