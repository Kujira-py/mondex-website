# MonDex
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React, TypeScript, clean CSS; the user's preferred stack. Local mock state, no backend.
## Users
Pokémon trading-card collectors testing a premium mobile collecting experience.
## Product Purpose
Browse and grow a card collection, discover Pokémon, complete collecting paths, scan demo cards, and optionally inspect values.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Five primary destinations: Home, Dex, Scan, Collection, Portfolio. A centered mobile application at maximum 430px width. Simulated scan and pre-grading only. Mock market values. Store prototype changes on this device.
## Brand Commitments
MonDex. Your cards first, their price second. Neutral dark interface; artwork supplies color. Restrained violet accent. Native, precise motion is central.
## Evidence on Hand
User's detailed 32-section product brief in pasted-text.txt. All numeric portfolio and collection data are demo data.
